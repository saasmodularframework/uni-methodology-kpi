# Assignmemtn-01

*Computer Lab Assignment · Stochastic Petri Nets Modeling & Simulation*

## Overview & Model Transfer

Use the **PNML** format to transfer the 4 Petri nets developed in the computer lab to the software for modeling stochastic Petri nets (**CPN IDE**, **PetriObjModelPaint**, **Renew**).

## Configuration Parameters

- **Transition Time Delays:** Add the necessary parameters for transition time delays.
- **Conflict Resolution:** Add parameters for resolving transition conflicts.

## Simulation & Evaluation Goals

Simulate the constructed models in order to evaluate the following numerical characteristics of the computational process:
- **Speed**
- **Productivity**
- **Amount of tasks accumulated in buffers**
