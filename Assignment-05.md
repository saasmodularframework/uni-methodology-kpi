# Assignment-05

*Computer Lab Assignment · Parallel Computations & Timed Petri Net Simulation*

## Overview & Requirements

Develop a simulation model for matrix multiplication using a thread pool, based on a timed Petri net. Employ established parallel matrix multiplication algorithms, such as Fox’s algorithm, Cannon’s algorithm, or strip-based methods. The matrices must contain at least **250,000 elements**. Alternatively, one may develop a model of parallel computations relevant to the specific research topic of the dissertation.
